# Round Rock Bitcoiners: Private Club Membership Model

## What We Are

A Texas unincorporated nonprofit association under Tex. Bus. Orgs. Code Ch. 252 — a Bitcoin meetup group governed by its members, with no corporate overhead, no single point of control, and a transparent on-chain treasury.

## How Spending Works

All funds are held in a 3-of-5 Bitcoin multisig wallet. No money moves without three signers agreeing. This is the only binding governance mechanism — everything else is discussion.

| Layer | Tool | Binding? |
|---|---|---|
| Discussion | Telegram, Signal, meetups | Never |
| Consensus | Polls, show of hands | Never |
| Spending | Multisig (3-of-5) | Always |

## Who's Involved

- **Signers**: Hold multisig keys. Trusted by the group. Rotate over time by consensus.
- **Contributors**: Documented participation in treasury or labor. Tracked in Git by pseudonym or Bitcoin address.
- **Participants**: Anyone who shows up. No dues, no tracking required.

## What Gets Documented in Git

- All spending proposals and outcomes
- Meeting notes
- Contributor participation history
- Links between treasury transactions and proposals

## Member Tax Notice

Contributing appreciated BTC to the treasury is a personal taxable event (capital gains) for the contributing member. Each member is responsible for their own tax obligations.

## Operating Budget

Monthly expenses run approximately $200-$400 (venue, food, misc.). Target reserve is 6 months of operating expenses. Contributions are voluntary; suggested 0.001 BTC per quarter.

## One Rule

If three signers won't sign it, it doesn't happen. When in doubt, do nothing.
